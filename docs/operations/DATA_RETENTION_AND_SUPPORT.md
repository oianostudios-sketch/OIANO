# OIANO Data Retention and Support Standard

Operational defaults below require counsel confirmation for every launch jurisdiction.

| Record | Default retention | Disposal |
|---|---:|---|
| Account/profile | Account life + 30 days | Delete or irreversibly anonymize unless legally retained |
| Booking/session operational data | 7 years | Secure deletion |
| Payments, ledger and invoices | 7 years | Restricted archive then secure deletion |
| Rights decisions, credits and consent evidence | 7 years after project closure; longer where an active dispute requires | Restricted archive |
| Authentication/security logs | 12 months | Rolling deletion |
| Support tickets | 24 months after closure | Delete attachments first where no longer required |
| Unaccepted invitations | 90 days | Delete personal contact data |
| Backups | 35 daily / 12 monthly | Cryptographic or provider deletion |

Users may request access, correction, export or deletion through `privacy@oiano.net`. Identity must be verified before disclosure or deletion. Financial, fraud, rights-dispute and legal-hold records are excluded only to the extent law requires.

Support uses `support@oiano.net`, assigns a ticket ID, records affected role/workflow and avoids requesting passwords, MFA codes or complete payment-card data. Security reports use `security@oiano.net` and are escalated under the incident plan.
