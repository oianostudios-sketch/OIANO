**The owner confirmed person-or-organization identification, with services offered by either, and separate evidence-backed network signals rather than one strength score. This records product direction, not implemented functionality.**

Clarified by [the later owner-approved universal Node doctrine](./2026-10-30-universal-node-expression-weave-doctrine.md): structural types must not become mandatory creative self-classifications; Node is a shared reference preserving distinct domain records.

## Confirmed decisions

- The identification journey begins with a creative person or creative organization. Either can offer creative services.
- A service is an offering associated with its provider, not a third authentication identity.
- People describe their practice through disciplines and self-description. Their role in a project is contextual, not a permanent account identity.
- Network signals remain separate: completed work, accepted collaborations, verified-service reviews, recency and availability. There is no combined node-strength score.

The owner selected both options explicitly during the 30 October clarification. This refines the journey consistently with [the frozen architecture](../OIANO_FROZEN_ARCHITECTURE.md); it does not change that document or waive its migration order.

## Design consequences and boundaries

- SKY is contextual discovery over real requirements. Its proposed network view can distinguish people, organizations, spaces and service offerings; an organization is not interchangeable with a physical creative space.
- Weave relationships must emerge from meaningful work and evidence, not follows or visual proximity. A displayed relationship needs an explainable source.
- Accepted participation is not completed work. Attribution is not proof of delivery. Availability is not reputation.
- Reviews must not be presented as verified until a defined verification rule links them to real service activity. Review eligibility, moderation, dispute handling and publication rules remain to be designed.
- Counts and dates must retain their context, provenance and visibility permissions. Missing evidence is unknown, not a zero-quality judgment.
- A future visual treatment of nodes must explain each signal separately. Node size, brightness or position must not silently introduce an overall ranking.

## Current implementation boundary

Today's Artist and Creative Professional signup paths remain unchanged. Public organization signup, canonical identity migration, service-offering models and a SKY tab were not implemented by this clarification.

The [stabilization gate](../OIANO_STABILIZATION_GATE.md) still requires production parity and complete sign-off before canonical migration. After that gate, identity and organization work must follow the frozen order; SKY follows its Work, Contribution, Requirement and Agreement prerequisites.
