## What changed

Recorded the approved doctrine in [the frozen architecture](../OIANO_FROZEN_ARCHITECTURE.md):

- Participants define themselves through open, editable expression rather than mandatory creative classifications.
- Expression, offering and intention evolve independently.
- A Node references its domain subject; it does not replace that subject's ownership, representation, lifecycle or authorization rules.
- Intelligence remains optional assistance, distinguishable from authored claims and verified records.
- Capabilities depend on authorized actions, with no duplicate accounts required merely to exercise different capabilities.
- Weave connects contextual work and governed evidence, not presumed endorsement or opaque rankings.
- NOW, MAKE, SKY and ME are interoperable perspectives rather than sequential participant stages.
- Implementation must reuse appropriate contracts and prove real multi-participant journeys, including incomplete, declined and disputed work.

The owner explicitly selected: “Adopt the doctrine; Node is a shared network reference, preserving distinct domain records (recommended)”.

This supersedes any reading of [the earlier identity-direction record](./2026-10-30-creative-network-identity-direction.md) that would make person-or-organization selection a mandatory creative self-classification. It preserves the distinction between provider identity and service offering, and the decision against a single node-strength score.

## Evidence and current boundary

Inspected the current schema and frozen laws before proposing the structural choice. Today's `WeaveNode` supports `ARTIST` and `STUDIO`; `ServiceOffering` belongs to a studio. Those existing records are not an implemented universal identity or offering system.

Documentation-only change, made after explicit owner approval. No schema, permissions, signup paths, financial behavior, migration workflow or production settings changed. No runtime tests were needed.
