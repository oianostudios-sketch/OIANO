# OIANO Platform Fee Standard

Fees are configured per studio in basis points (`platform_fee_bps`; 100 basis points = 1%). The default is 0 until a signed studio commercial schedule authorizes another value.

Checkout must display gross booking price, taxes where applicable, customer-facing fees and refund terms. The financial ledger allocates every settled payment between studio payable and OIANO platform revenue. Partial/full refunds reverse both allocations proportionally.

Fee changes apply only prospectively, require authorized OIANO administration, must be audited and must not alter historical bookings. Payout timing, reserves, chargebacks and taxes are governed by the studio agreement.
