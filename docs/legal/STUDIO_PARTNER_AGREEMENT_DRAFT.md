# OIANO Studio Partner Agreement — Counsel Review Draft

The studio confirms authority to list its facilities, accurate pricing/availability, qualified personnel, safe and lawful premises, required licences/insurance and timely service delivery. It must protect user information, use OIANO data only for booked services and report incidents promptly.

OIANO provides discovery, booking, workflow, payment collection, project coordination and business reporting. OIANO does not control the studio’s professional performance or employ its personnel.

Commercial schedule per studio must state currency, `platform_fee_bps`, taxes, payout timing, minimum payout, refund allocation, chargeback responsibility and reserves. No fee is activated until both parties accept the schedule. OIANO’s ledger records payable amounts; payout execution and bank verification are separate controls.

The final agreement must cover service levels, cancellations, intellectual property, confidentiality, data processing, prohibited conduct, audit rights, suspension, indemnities, liability, term/termination and governing law. Entity details and signature blocks require counsel.
