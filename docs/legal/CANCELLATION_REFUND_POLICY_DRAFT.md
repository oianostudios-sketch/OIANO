# OIANO Cancellation and Refund Policy — Business Approval Draft

The exact cancellation window and fee must be shown before booking. Until OIANO approves a universal commercial rule, each studio must publish its own terms and OIANO must record the version accepted at checkout.

Recommended pilot default: full refund at least 48 hours before session start; 50% refund from 24–48 hours; no automatic refund within 24 hours. A studio cancellation receives a full refund. Documented service failure is reviewed case by case. Consumer-law rights override this policy.

Refunds return to the original payment method where possible. Wallet-funded refunds return to wallet liability only after the refund transaction is posted. Processing times depend on the provider. Platform-fee treatment, no-show rules, rescheduling limits, force majeure and chargeback handling require business and counsel approval.

Every refund must reference its booking, payment, reason, approving identity and balanced ledger reversal.
