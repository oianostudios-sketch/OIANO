# Rights, Credits and Professional Records Notice

OIANO distinguishes contribution, credit, consent and ownership.

- A contribution invitation documents a proposed role.
- An accepted credit confirms how a person contributed; it does not itself transfer copyright, master ownership or publishing rights.
- Promotional consent permits only the stated assets, channels, purpose and duration.
- A rights proposal becomes effective in OIANO only after every identity-bound holder approves their named share.

OIANO preserves authenticated decisions and timestamps but does not determine whether a proposed split is legally correct, register copyrights or replace independent legal advice. Users should resolve disputes and obtain professional advice before release, licensing, distribution or royalty collection.
