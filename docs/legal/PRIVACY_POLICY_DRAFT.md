# OIANO Privacy Policy — Counsel Review Draft

Controller/entity, registration address, representative and applicable-law disclosures: **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]**.

OIANO processes account identity and contact data; artist, producer, engineer and studio profiles; bookings and project activity; messages; files and deliverable metadata; credits, consent and rights decisions; payment references and ledger records; security, device and audit data; and support communications.

Purposes include providing accounts and role permissions, fulfilling bookings, processing payments, coordinating projects, creating professional records, preventing fraud, securing the service, supporting users, complying with law and improving aggregate service performance. Legal bases vary by jurisdiction and may include contract, consent, legitimate interests and legal obligation.

Data is shared only with project/studio participants according to permissions, payment and infrastructure processors, professional advisers and authorities where law requires. OIANO does not sell personal data. International-transfer mechanisms, processor list, cookie disclosures and age threshold require jurisdiction-specific confirmation.

Users may request access, correction, portability, objection, restriction or deletion at `privacy@oiano.net`. OIANO verifies identity and may retain payment, fraud, rights and dispute evidence where legally required. Retention follows the repository retention standard. Security incidents are handled under the incident response plan.
