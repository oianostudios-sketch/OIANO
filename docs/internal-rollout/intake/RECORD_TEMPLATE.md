# Intake record template

One copy per person per example piece of work. **Keep completed records outside this
repository** (it is public). See README.md for where they go. Only anonymised patterns
move into `SYNTHESIS.md`.

Keep the four layers separate. Never edit one into another:

- **Told:** what the person said, in their words, with the question number. Quote
  when you can. Do not tidy their vocabulary into ours.
- **Today:** what OIANO does now for this, from `CURRENT_SUPPORT.md`. "Not supported"
  is a valid entry.
- **Proposed:** our inference or suggestion, marked as ours. Can be empty.
- **Confirmed:** the person's own response to the proposal, with the date. Until
  then, write "not yet shown".
