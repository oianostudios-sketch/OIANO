Routes:
1. **Supported**: it already works; tell the person how.
2. **Temporary agreement**: accepted limitation, recorded in their record.
3. **Small fix**: fits today's model without a new concept; propose it to the owner.
4. **Canonical step N**: belongs to a frozen-architecture concept; feed it into that
   step's design, do not build it ahead of the order.
5. **More intake**: not clear yet; ask the follow-up.
