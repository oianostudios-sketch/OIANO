# Creative work primitives intake

> 2026-10-27: the working method is now [intake/](intake/README.md), with a participant questionnaire, a private record template, a check of current support and a synthesis sheet. This page stays as the original outline.

Complete one copy per person, using one recent real piece of work. A person may have several disciplines and different responsibilities on different projects. Their answers are authoritative statements of need; proposed system mappings require their confirmation.

1. What do you call your practice, and what are you making in this example?
2. Who starts the work, who joins, and what does each person contribute?
3. What inputs do you need before you can begin: brief, reference, script, footage, sound, location, equipment, budget or something else?
4. What are the actual steps and handoffs? Use your own words.
5. What outputs and file versions do you deliver? Who can see each one?
6. Who reviews the result, how do they request changes, and what means accepted or finished?
7. What appointments, rooms, locations, people or equipment must be coordinated?
8. What terms, credit, usage permission or payment must be agreed? Who must accept them?
9. What should remain private? What happens if someone leaves the work?
10. What should OIANO remind you about? Which choices should it never make without you?
11. What currently wastes your time, and which one improvement would make OIANO useful this week?

Candidate shared primitives to validate: work, brief, contribution, requirement, appointment, resource, deliverable, version, review, agreement, credit and evidence. Do not force every profession to follow identical steps. A film deliverable might need cut/version/review; a poem might need draft/performance/publication. Those are interview prompts, not established requirements.

Keep identity, project responsibility and permission separate. For example, a cinematographer's discipline does not automatically grant access to every studio or the ability to approve everyone's deliverables. Preserve who supplied each fact, when it was confirmed and what remains an assistant suggestion.
